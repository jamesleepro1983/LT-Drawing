(A) THE PRACTICE

Small practice.
Straight answers.
Solid approvals.

LT Drawing Services is owned and run by Jason from Ickham, just outside Canterbury. Every project gets his direct attention — from talking through the options on day one to the final sign-off.

We help clients understand what’s possible early, compare design options honestly and make informed decisions on cost. Our track record of planning and building consent approvals is backed by references from satisfied customers across Kent.

(B) SERVICES

Conception to completion.

Our service doesn’t stop at the drawing board. We manage the applications for you and can stay on through the build.

ARCHITECTURAL

Planning applications

Measured surveys, design options and full planning drawings — prepared, submitted and managed with the local authority on your behalf.

Building regulations

Detailed technical drawings and specifications for Building Control, ready for your builder to price and construct from.

SURVEYING

Employer’s agent

Independent representation on your side of the contract — tendering, valuations and keeping the build aligned with what was agreed.

Project management

Coordinating trades, programme and budget so your project runs smoothly from start on site to handover.

(C) PROCESS

Four stages. One point of contact.

  1. 01

    Initial ideas

    A site visit and an honest conversation about what you want, what’s achievable and what it might cost.

  2. 02

    Design options

    Survey and sketch schemes, refined together until the design fits your brief and budget.

  3. 03

    Submission

    Planning and building regulations drawings prepared, submitted and seen through to approval.

  4. 04

    Construction

    Optional support on site as employer’s agent or project manager, through to completion.
